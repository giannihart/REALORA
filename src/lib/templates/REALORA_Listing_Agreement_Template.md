This Exclusive Listing Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [EFFECTIVE_DATE] between [SELLER_LEGAL_NAME] ("Seller") and [BROKERAGE_LEGAL_NAME], a licensed New York real estate broker, License No. [BROKERAGE_LICENSE] ("Brokerage").

1. Parties and property
Seller is the owner of the property located at [PROPERTY_ADDRESS] (Tax Block [BLOCK], Lot [LOT]; BBL [BBL]) (the "Property"). Brokerage's broker of record is [BROKER_OF_RECORD_NAME], License No. [BROKER_OF_RECORD_LICENSE]. All fixtures are included in the sale except as noted in the listing; personal property is included only as set out in the listing.

2. Exclusive right to sell
Seller grants Brokerage the sole and exclusive right to sell, trade, convey, or exchange the Property during the Listing Period on the terms of this Agreement. Seller appoints Brokerage as exclusive agent and will refer all inquiries, offers, and negotiations concerning the Property to Brokerage during the Listing Period.

3. Term and protection period
This Agreement begins on [EFFECTIVE_DATE] and ends at 12:00 midnight on [EXPIRATION_DATE] (the "Listing Period"), unless extended in writing. If the Property is sold, conveyed, exchanged, optioned, or otherwise transferred within [PROTECTION_DAYS] days after expiration to any person with whom Brokerage negotiated during the Listing Period, the compensation in §4 is due, unless the Property was in good faith listed with another brokerage during that period. "Negotiated" includes providing information about the Property, showing the Property, or presenting an offer.

4. Compensation
As compensation for its services, Brokerage shall receive one percent (1%) of the purchase price, payable at closing. The amount or rate of real estate commissions is not fixed by law; the commission is set by each broker individually and may be negotiable between Seller and Brokerage. If, during the Listing Period, Brokerage produces a buyer who is ready, willing, and able to purchase the Property on the price and terms set out in the listing or on other terms the Seller accepts, and the Seller refuses the transaction without legal cause, the commission is deemed earned on the same terms. If a suit is brought to collect compensation under this Agreement and Brokerage prevails, Seller agrees to pay Brokerage's reasonable costs of the action, including attorneys' fees.

5. Cooperation and buyer's-agent compensation

Seller does not offer compensation to a buyer's agent under this Agreement. Brokerage represents buyers on this Property at no fee to the buyer. If a buyer is represented by an outside agent, any compensation to that agent is a matter between that buyer and their agent unless the Seller agrees otherwise in writing in connection with an accepted offer.

Seller elects to offer one percent (1%) of the purchase price to a licensed buyer's agent who procures the buyer whose offer the Seller accepts and who completes the transaction, payable at closing. This election is in addition to Brokerage's compensation in §4, which remains 1%. Brokerage will communicate this offer on the Property's listing pages; consistent with current rules, it is not published in the RLS/MLS compensation fields.

6. Designated agency
Brokerage represents the Seller under this Agreement. Brokerage's model is to represent both sides of a transaction on its platform. If Brokerage also represents the buyer of this Property, the transaction will proceed only under designated agency: Brokerage's broker of record will designate one licensed agent (or the broker of record) to represent the Seller and a different licensed agent to represent the buyer, the broker of record will supervise both, and Brokerage will first obtain the informed written consent of both Seller and buyer. No confidential information of one party will be disclosed to the other without that party's consent, except as required by law. Brokerage receives no compensation from the buyer; its compensation is solely as set out in §§4–5. This Agreement is not a buyer-broker agreement. The New York Agency Disclosure Form is provided and signed separately, as required by law.

7. Marketing and media
Seller authorizes Brokerage to: advertise the Property and use its street address in advertising; publish all Property information in print and electronically, including the REBNY Residential Listing Service (RLS), the MLS where applicable, and third-party websites (including StreetEasy, Zillow, and Trulia); disclose the existence of other offers on the Property; and arrange photographic services — interior and exterior photography, video, and 3D tours — and to reproduce, publish, and distribute that media in any form to market the Property. Seller waives and releases Brokerage and cooperating licensees from liability arising from such media and its distribution. A lockbox and a "For Sale" sign will be used only if Seller elects them in the listing. Seller may prohibit publication of transfer information only by a separate written addendum, as RLS/MLS rules allow.

8. Brokerage's duties
Brokerage owes Seller fiduciary duties of loyalty, obedience, and full disclosure, and will use reasonable efforts to sell the Property at the best price and terms obtainable. Brokerage will: produce the Property's media; distribute the listing through the RLS and the websites in §7; present every offer to Seller through the platform, with its terms and the verification the platform has performed on the buyer; and coordinate showings, inspections, and the parties' professionals through closing. Attorneys for the parties — not Brokerage — draft the contract of sale and closing instruments; this Agreement does not itself transfer title or create a binding sale.

9. Seller's duties
Seller will make reasonable efforts to accommodate showings, open houses, inspections, and appraisals. Seller will consider in good faith all offers presented by Brokerage. Seller alone determines the price at which the Property is offered. The asking price is recorded in the listing — not in this Agreement — and may be changed on Seller's written instruction to Brokerage, which Seller may give through the platform at any time.

10. Deposits and escrow
Brokerage never holds client money. All earnest-money and contract deposits are held by the Seller's attorney in escrow, in accordance with the contract of sale. Brokerage does not act as escrow agent for any party's funds. Any dispute over a deposit is between buyer and seller under the contract of sale.

11. Seller's representations and acknowledgments
Seller warrants that Seller is the owner of the Property (or has full authority to act for the owner), has complete authority to execute this Agreement and sell the Property, and that the Property Disclosure Statement and all information Seller provides are accurate and complete to the best of Seller's knowledge. Unless otherwise disclosed to Brokerage in writing, Seller is unaware of: any default recorded against the Property; any financial delinquency secured by the Property; any bankruptcy or insolvency involving Seller or the Property; or any litigation, mediation, arbitration, or proceeding pending against the Property or affecting Seller's ability to sell. Seller will notify Brokerage immediately if any of these arise during the Listing Period. Seller agrees to indemnify and hold Brokerage harmless from claims arising from false information Seller provides, including reimbursement of Brokerage's reasonable attorneys' fees in any resulting dispute.

12. Property condition disclosure
As required by New York law (Real Property Law Article 14), Seller will complete the Property Condition Disclosure Statement truthfully and completely, and Brokerage will provide it to prospective buyers as the law requires. Seller authorizes Brokerage and cooperating licensees to disclose defects known to them, latent or otherwise, to the extent required by law; licensees have no duty to discover latent defects.

13. Lead-based paint
If any structure on the Property was constructed before January 1, 1978, Seller will comply with the federal lead-based paint disclosure requirements (42 U.S.C. §4852d): providing the EPA-approved pamphlet, disclosing known lead-based paint and hazards, providing available records, and affording the buyer the statutory inspection period unless waived in writing.

14. Fair housing
Brokerage and Seller will comply with all applicable federal, state, and local fair housing laws. The Property will be shown and offered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, military status, age, marital status, source of income, or any other class protected by law.

15. Insurance; other clients
Seller will maintain hazard or other insurance on the Property, current as of the Effective Date, until the Property is sold. Seller acknowledges that Brokerage may represent other clients with similar properties and will not favor any client's property over the Property.

Seller consents to conduct this transaction electronically. This Agreement may be executed by legally recognized electronic signature, which is binding and has the same force and effect as an original ink signature, and in counterparts. Notices under this Agreement may be given through the platform or by email to the addresses the parties have provided, and are effective on confirmed delivery.

## Adaptation notes (for counsel — not part of the agreement)

Removed from the source template, and why

- §IV Purchase Price — deleted. Per the Nov 4 flow decision, the agreement is signed before the listing exists; price lives in the listing only. References to "Purchase Price" as a defined term were neutralized (§4's RWA clause now keys off "the price and terms set out in the listing"). §9 preserves the template's one good price sentence in adapted form: the seller alone determines the price.
- §XX Earnest Money — replaced outright. The template authorized the agency to hold buyer funds in escrow. REALORA never holds client money; deposits sit with the seller's attorney. This was the template's most dangerous clause for this model.
- §VI(a) Leasing — deleted. No rental business.
- §VI(b) Deed Type — deleted. Deed type belongs to the attorneys' contract of sale in New York practice.
- §XI Non-refundable payment split — deleted. The template split forfeited deposits with the agency; with attorney-held escrow and attorney-drafted contracts, forfeiture is governed by the contract of sale, and the brokerage claims nothing outside earned commission (§10 carries the operative rule).
- Per-page initials — removed. Paper artifact; the e-signature platform's audit trail supersedes it.

Rewritten

- §VI/§VII Commission & cooperation → §§4–5. Fixed 1% (the negotiability sentence is retained verbatim — it is legally protective). The template let the agency share commission "as determined solely by the Agency"; here the buyer's-agent 1% is the seller's election, rendered as Variant A/B from the election recorded on the signing step.
- §VIII Dual Agency → §6 Designated Agency. The template consented to classic dual agency and let the broker "collect compensation from the Buyer" — both contradict the model (0% buyer fee; separate designated agents under broker supervision, informed written consent first). NY's separate Agency Disclosure Form is acknowledged, not replaced.
- §XIV MLS → §7 RLS-first. New York City distribution runs through REBNY's RLS with syndication to StreetEasy/Zillow/Trulia; the template's generic MLS language was adapted, keeping the seller's publication opt-out by addendum.
- §XXII Lead paint — corrected. The template had the seller represent the home was not built before 1978 — false for nearly every NYC townhouse. §13 is now a conditional compliance clause.
- §IX Marketing initials-menu → §7 grants. Photography/video/3D authorization and the media waiver are retained (the media model runs on them) as granted permissions; lockbox and sign became elections recorded in the listing.

Added (not in the template)

- Designated-agency machinery (§6), the never-holds-money rule (§10), attorneys draft the contract (§8), price-is-the-seller's-alone + lives in the listing (§9), platform duties (§8: media, distribution, verified offer presentation), e-signature consent (§17), source of income in fair housing (§14 — NY-protected class the template omitted).

Open decisions flagged in the text

- §16 Termination — the template had none. A termination right is included bracketed; enforceability against the protection period is counsel's call.
- §18 Dispute resolution — the template imposed binding AAA arbitration. Bracketed: arbitration vs. NY courts is a business/trust decision, not a drafting default.
- §12 PCDS — counsel to confirm the current statutory statement, including the 2024 amendments (flood-history questions; the former $500-credit alternative).
- Protection period length (§3) — [PROTECTION_DAYS] is a variable; set the number (90 is common) as a business term.
